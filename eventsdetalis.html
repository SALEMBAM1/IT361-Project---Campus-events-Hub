Artificial Intelligence Workshop

Learn the fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence through an engaging hands-on workshop organized by the Student Activities Department.



Date: July 25, 2026

Time: 4:00 PM - 6:00 PM

Location: Computer Science Building, Lab 3

Category: Academic & Career

Organizer: Student Activities Department

Capacity: 50 Students


Event Description

This workshop introduces students to the basics of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. Participants will explore real-world AI applications, complete simple practical exercises, and learn about future career opportunities in this growing field. The event concludes with a live demonstration and an open question-and-answer session with faculty members.
